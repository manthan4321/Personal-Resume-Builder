Commitment to Accessibility

Our Commitment

ResumeNow is committed to providing expert employment advice, professional resume and cover letter guidance, resources, and tools accessible to everyone who needs them, including persons with disabilities.

We ensure that our content complies with federal regulations under the Rehabilitation Act, and we will continue to follow Website Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to provide web pages that are WCAG Level A compliant.

Giving access to valuable tools and information on a platform that meets the needs of all job seekers, regardless of their circumstances, is of the utmost importance to ResumeNow. We remain committed to accessibility for all.

Recommended Screen Reader and Browser Combinations

To improve your ResumeNow.com experience, we recommend the following screen reader and browser combinations:

  • Windows users: Try NVDA and Chrome or Edge
  • Mac users: Try VoiceOver and Safari
  • Mobile users: We suggest VoiceOver for iPhone and TalkBack for Android devices
